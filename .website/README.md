# RAINBOW RENEGADES

As warriors in the service of light, you must free the world from the darkness unleashed by the Unicorn King after he stole the Gem of Light.

Find him in his sealed lair, locked behind the seven Rainbow Gems. Destroy the totems, recover the gems, open the door, defeat the Unicorn King, and save the world.

## CONTROLS

- `[Arrow Keys]` - Move
- `[Space]` - Attack / destroy totems / interact with the door

To collect items, simply walk over them.

## SINGLE OR MULTIPLAYER

Play alone or team up with friends.

Choose `HOST` to create a room and share its code so your friends can join your adventure. Or host a room and begin the quest solo as a lone warrior of light.

## NOTES

- If you die during the mission, do not worry. You will respawn at the beginning and can continue your adventure until you complete it.
